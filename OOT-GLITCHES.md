# Ocarina of Time (Switch 2) glitches

The glitches known in the 2026 Ocarina of Time remake as of 26 November 2026.

- **Sources:** the Ocarina of Time 2026 speedrunning Discord (glitch-hunting channel, the routing forum and the per-glitch video threads), the ZSR wiki, StratDB, YouTube videos and run commentary. Links are in [Sources](#sources).
- **Dates** are UTC, usually the day the first clip or thread was posted.
- **Credits** use the names people post under. Where a thread or video names a finder, that is what is listed.
- **"Works on"** lists the versions someone reported it working on, from thread tags and posts. "1.0 only" means it was reported not to work on 1.1.1. What patch 1.1.2 changed is in [its own section](#patch-112-26-nov).
- **Any% impact** is for runs from a fresh file (no NG+) on 1.0 or 1.1.1, judged against the route in use on 26 November.

## Patch 1.1.2 (26 Nov)

Version 1.1.2 came out at about 01:20 UTC on 26 Nov. Its notes only say that several issues were fixed; the one named fix is a softlock in Malon's Epona quest (*Tustin2121*). *Linkus7* tested it live on stream that night. No dump of 1.1.2 exists yet, because the current console firmware closes the exploit used for dumping (*Volvagia*, *nozzly*), so everything below comes from console play.

- **Fixed:** the [jumpslash cancel](#jumpslash-cancel) ("the game now tracks your height properly", *nozzly*; confirmed by *lastlevi*, *SilverWind*, *Izagar* and *Kyle_Speedruns*) and the [actor jump](#actor-jump) (*nozzly*, *SilverWind*, *Ben - BJL*). Jump slashes in combat are unchanged. With them go the Shadow Temple entry by hover and everything that starts with one: the Song of Time, Iron Boots and Longshot skips, the boss key skips' hovers and the Hyrule Field [Forest Escape](#forest-escape) (that last one inferred, not tested).
- **Reported fixed, less sure:** the [bomb T-pose](#bomb-t-pose) (*SilverWind*, *DrLight66*, no tester named), the [Hover Boots bottle extension](#hover-boots-bottle-extension) (seen failing on *Linkus7*'s stream), the [Gold Skulltula respawn](#smaller-finds) (one test by *Piggachew*), the [Yiga Warp](#yiga-warp) (hearsay) and the [hover jump](#hover-jump) (*sep*, unsure).
- **Still working:** [chest minigame storage](#chest-minigame-storage) (*nozzly*, *SilverWind*, *sep*), [BOoB](#bottle-out-of-bounds-boob) (*Ben - BJL*, *UrbanGarlic*), the [puberty skip](#puberty-skip) (*UrbanGarlic*, from a 1.1.1 save at the Spirit blue warp), the Light Arrow cutscene's check for only the Shadow and Spirit medallions (*Vidya James*), the [Deku Tree B1 skip](#deku-tree-b1-skip) (*Sora*), the item-swap animation cancel, the [cucco teleport](#cucco-teleport) and the adult Bottom of the Well entry (*Piggachew*, *DrLight66*), the [actor despawn](#actor-despawn) (*Piggachew*) and the [Geddan boost](#geddan-boost) (*Dalton-ico*). Second-hand: the [Jabu Ruto teleport](#jabu-ruto-teleport).
- **Unchanged:** boss health and cycles (*Sora*, after two runs through the major bosses). A faster sprint was claimed and is unproven.
- **What it means:** 1.1.2 Any% is for now "likely just glitchless with boob shadow temple" (*Vespher*); *nozzly*: the current patch "has no glitches besides chest storage and boob". 1.1.1 can no longer be downloaded, so a new player can only run 1.0 (a cartridge with the update removed by a factory reset) or 1.1.2. A save from a newer version stops an older one from starting it (*Lid*). Many runners turned automatic updates off. *Tustin2121* keeps a [list of version differences](https://github.com/tustin2121/OoTR-saveTree/wiki/Version-Differences).

## Any% progression history

### The first run (6 Nov, 4:28:55)

The baseline is the first run uploaded, [4:28:55 by *gymnast86*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmtJQD3y_nY). It uses no glitches: it plays the normal game in order, and its only saving is skipping the Lens of Truth by knowing where the hidden things are.

1. **Child:** Deku Tree, meet Zelda, Dodongo's Cavern, Jabu-Jabu. The Goron's Ruby and Zora's Sapphire trigger Zelda's escape, which gives the Ocarina of Time and the Song of Time. The song and all three stones open the Door of Time, and pulling the Master Sword makes Link an adult.
2. **Adult:** Hookshot from Dampé, then the Forest Temple (bow), Fire Temple (Megaton Hammer), Ice Cavern (Iron Boots) and Water Temple (Longshot). Finishing Forest, Fire and Water unlocks the Nocturne of Shadow, the warp to the Shadow Temple. The Spirit Temple needs a trip back to childhood.
3. **End:** the Shadow and Spirit medallions trigger the Light Arrow cutscene and the bridge to Ganondorf's Castle. Then six trials, Ganondorf, the tower collapse (which needs the Longshot) and Ganon.

### The route around 9 Nov (about 3 hours)

Still all three child dungeons, the Door of Time and the Master Sword. Jumpslash cancels, found on 7 Nov, were already used all through the run, and the savings were mostly on the adult side. This is the route of [*Linkus7*'s breakdown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OUd8RQ_zd2Q) of 9 Nov, which puts the record then at "right around 3 hours":

- **Shadow Temple without the Nocturne.** Actor and bean jumps first, then hammer jumpslash cancels, reach the entrance directly, so the Forest, Fire and Water temples no longer had to be finished.
- **Those temples were still entered** for their items: bow, hammer and Longshot. The Ice Cavern was still needed for the Iron Boots. The Longshot and Iron Boots were needed only for one gap in the tower collapse.
- **Gerudo Fortress skipped** by a hover out of bounds past the guards.
- **Spirit Temple from the adult side only**, jumping straight to the Mirror Shield.

## High impact

### Jumpslash cancel

- **Also known as:** JSC, IJSC (infinite jumpslash cancel), IJS, infinite jump, infinite height glitch, super jump, sword jump, Deku Stick jump, hammer hover, hovering.
- **What it does:** a jump slash started in mid-air gives Link the take-off speed of a fresh 0.7 m jump, as long as he is still rising. A slash cannot restart itself, but changing the item on the weapon button ends it at once and Link drops into a fall, and from a fall the next press starts a new jump slash. So the chain climbs for as long as it is kept up.
- **How:** target and jump slash with a Deku Stick or the Megaton Hammer, open the item wheel, pick another item, let the game run for at least one frame (the slash ends on the first running frame after the wheel closes), pick the weapon again, close the wheel and press the weapon button, then reopen the wheel before Link stops rising. Repeat. The weapon only has to be back on its button, not drawn. The sword does not work: a sword slash cannot be ended in the air.
- **Height cap:** a normal jump stores the height it will peak at (take-off height + 0.7 m), and mid-air jump slashes lift Link up to that height and no further. In the game's code a sidehop sets the same cap; backflips and jump slashes from the ground do not. Returning to the title screen clears it, and so does any change of age (*Poodleskirt*, 17 Nov), which includes the blue warps of the puberty skip. The cap is an absolute height that carries between maps, and maps sit at different heights: a jump in Hyrule Field sets a cap high enough to hover to the sky in Gerudo Fortress. Route sheets mark where to jump before each hover so the stored height is enough for it, and fights on the way are done without jumping to keep it. The same stored height is what the [fall damage cancel](#fall-damage-cancel) uses.
- **Gain per cancel:** in the game's code it is set by how many frames the game runs between two slashes: 0.43 m with 4, 0.59 m with 7, up to 0.7 m with 12, and nothing once Link has stopped rising.
- **Menu order:** sorting the item wheel by most recent makes the menu inputs the same on every cancel (*Edd* and *nozzly*, 16 Nov).
- **Variants:**
  - *Backwards jumpslash* (*bloonstd6master*, 10 Nov): with no sword equipped, target, hold back, press A and tap ZL twice. The ZSR wiki starts it from a backhop and releases the target button once the chain is going. Link hovers while moving backwards, which gains a lot of height in a small space, and holding target again turns it back into a forward hover three cancels later. The first Forest Escape setup used it.
  - *Faster start* (*Kenny-Dumminik*, 11 Nov): pressing the weapon button and A together while holding forward starts the hover without a first swing. *Kenny-Dumminik* wrote that the timing might be frame perfect.
  - *Pause method* (*flutterdashie64*, 8 Nov): the same chain driven by the pause button. In the game's code the pause menu changes the item through the same routine as the wheel.
  - *Higher hover* (*Tholive_* and *Ataappi*): a jump slash lets a Hover Boots hover start above the platform.
- **Found by:** *Ataappi*, 7 Nov. The mechanics were written up by *nathanisbored*.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. Hovering to the Shadow Temple entrance removed the need for the Nocturne of Shadow, and so for finishing the Forest, Fire and Water temples. It is also how child Link gets through that temple, and the Song of Time skip, Iron Boots skip, Longshot skip and Forest Escape all start with a hover.
- **Video:** [Jumpslash Cancel Glitch Tutorial by *gymnast86*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4HSZ6GMQCZk). Inputs with a hand camera: [*secondbaker*'s link](https://youtu.be/aaEfLhigIws).
- **As a script:** [research/infinite-jumpslash](research/infinite-jumpslash/README.md): the chain at 50 frames a loop (0.69 m up each), played inside the game by sloperinaGZ, with each timing value's window and the height formula.

### Actor jump

- **Also known as:** actor bouncing, ocarina jump, bean jump, bean flower jump, chest jump, child super jump, hookshot jump.
- **What it does:** jumping on the frame an aim or ocarina animation ends, while standing on a movable or interactive object, launches Link far higher than a normal jump. A moving object adds its speed.
- **How:** stand on an actor (chest, gravestone, box, block, bean plant), hold target, take out the ocarina, turn the camera all the way down, put the ocarina away and press jump a moment later. The hookshot, bow, slingshot and boomerang work in place of the ocarina. Child Link can do it with only the Hylian Shield, and *RoboRotembo* showed on 9 Nov that opening the item wheel works with no item at all. *Edd*'s summary (18 Nov) is that anything that forces the camera to snap works: the pause menu, a bottle, putting away an aimed hookshot. Hookshot jumps are shorter, and child Link gets less height than adult Link.
- **Variants:**
  - *Bean jump* (*Vidya James*, 6 Nov): the first form found, off a moving bean plant.
  - *Directional actor jump* (*Hellios*, 10 Nov): releasing the ocarina quickly steers the launch.
  - *Hitstop actor jump* (*RoboRotembo*, 16 Nov): see [Hitstop launches](#hitstop-launches).
  - *Big chests:* an open big chest touches Link on three sides and gives a larger launch, which depends on lineup and direction (*RoboRotembo*, *Vespher*).
  - *Menu actor jump:* targeting in the pause menu and jumping on unpause gives the highest plain actor jump, because the camera can tilt all the way under Link (*RoboRotembo*, 25 Nov; [tutorial by *Solarance*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzmF_jw5H0E)). Off the Fire Temple's moving platforms Link keeps their momentum (*Edd*).
- **Found by:** *Vidya James* (bean jump), then *Ataappi* showed it works on any actor and with the shield alone.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. It was the first way into the Shadow Temple early and to the Mirror Shield and Silver Gauntlets. Much of that is now done with jumpslash cancels. Runs still use actor jumps in the Fire Temple and, off a chest in the Shadow trial, to skip lifting the Golden Gauntlets block ([tutorial by *parallelograms*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yjS5MQpx4Tw)).
- **Video:** [Actor Jumping by *Kethaldo*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rop0oeZVk24). A fixed setup: [Bean Jump for Shadow Temple Early by *RoboRotembo*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1EIvnoPTQ48).

### Puberty skip

- **Also known as:** child skip, early adult, "the Door of Time skip".
- **What it does:** finishing an adult dungeon as child Link plays the sage cutscene and returns Link to the overworld as an adult, with the Kokiri Sword still equipped. The Door of Time never has to open. The Master Sword then arrives through the Light Arrow cutscene, which checks only for the Shadow and Spirit medallions.
- **Why it works:** the Shadow Temple's blue warp always loads the adult Graveyard map, and a map forces Link's age to match it. Current age and "has done the Master Sword sequence" are tracked separately, so Link is adult without that sequence ever running. *leo* found the setting in the map data (17 Nov): overworld maps are marked to force the age, dungeons and the Hall of Fallen Foes are not. *MaddoScientisto* and *Nova* read the blue warp scripts: every adult dungeon's warp makes a child adult, and only the Dodongo's Cavern warp checks age. Leaving the Gerudo Training Ground as a child also turns Link adult, because Gerudo Fortress has no child map. The Master Sword pedestal offers an age change once the "Forest Sage quest completed" flag is set, with no Prelude or medallion (*nozzly*, 22 Nov, set with sloperina). *nozzly* also read that finishing the Forest Temple or watching the Prelude sets the main quest back a step, which blocks the Light Arrow cutscene; Fire and Water only advance it.
- **Found by:** *oussama* and *tvbrayden*, per the ZSR wiki. *kittenchilly* mentioned it on 6 Nov and *tvbrayden* wrote it up on 7 Nov.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. On its own it replaced the Door of Time and Master Sword sequence. With it in the route the Spiritual Stones had one use left, the Song of Time, which is why the Song of Time skip could remove two child dungeons.
- **Video:** any Any% run since 9 Nov, for example the [1:44:30 record](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j3K8aSeeH3w).

### Save/load respawn manipulation

- **Also known as:** save warp, save/load clip, out of bounds to in bounds, loading a file in mid-air.
- **What it does:** a save stores Link's last safe position, and a load runs a second, stricter check. If Link saves somewhere the game does not accept, the load places him at a nearby valid spot, which can be on the other side of a wall. Playing the ocarina out of bounds pins the save position there.
- **How the game picks the spot** (*RoboRotembo* and *ontrigger*, 17 Nov): the respawn point follows Link only on stable ground in bounds, and the ocarina or a cutscene item forces it to update anywhere. The game then looks for the nearest valid spot to that point, preferring in bounds over out of bounds. So the trick can bring Link from out of bounds to in bounds through a wall, and cannot send him out. Farore's Wind set out of bounds behaves like the ocarina.
- **Found by:** *wxyz* (9 Nov, out of bounds back in bounds), *GlitchesAndStuff* (9 Nov, loading in mid-air), *Poodleskirt* (10 Nov, loading into a place never visited), *Dusty_Carpet* (13 Nov, ocarina pins the position).
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. The Song of Time skip and the Iron Boots skip are both built on it.
- **Video:** [Ocarina saving our position from out of bounds by *Dusty_Carpet*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRGNLxWWfDc).

### Bomb T-pose

- **Also known as:** bomb pose (the ZSR wiki's name), bomb/chu T-pose, bomb clip.
- **What it does:** putting the ocarina away while Link is breathing in leaves his upper-body animation in a wider state. Pulling a bomb or bombchu while moving then swings his arms out and pushes him through nearby collision.
- **How:** hold any note until it wobbles (3 seconds or more), release it, wait half a second, put the ocarina away, then pull a bomb while moving. *Nova* found that any of Link's inhales works if the put-away is timed to it. Jumping, climbing, rolling, sliding, throwing or picking up a bomb, pulling one while standing still or taking damage ends the state.
- **Found by:** *Pasty* (*PastyPixel*), 12 Nov.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high, as the clip used in the Song of Time skip.
- **Video:** [Any% Is Now 20 Minutes Faster by *ThreeLetterName*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4V1lRs6J-Sk), which shows the T-pose and the skip.

### Song of Time skip

- **Also known as:** SoT skip, SoT block skip.
- **What it does:** the bow is in the Forest Temple, behind a block that only the Song of Time removes. This skip reaches the east courtyard without the song. Hover to the top of a column in the main room, save, do the bomb T-pose to wedge Link into the corner, save again and reload: Link loads into the courtyard on the other side of the wall. The bow was then reached from the west courtyard, across a flooded well; the [Iron Boots skip](#iron-boots-skip-invalid-respawn) now goes straight to it.
- **Found by:** *Ultraspeed7* found the save/load spot (11 Nov), *Pasty* the T-pose, and *ontrigger* posted the first full clip on 12 Nov. Later setups by *Lid*, *Layz*, *jakezsr* and *CrypticJacknife* cut the hover from about 60 cancels to under 20, and a "zero-lineup" setup needs about 6 early-timed cancels.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. Learning the song takes the Goron's Ruby and Zora's Sapphire, and this block was its only use, so Dodongo's Cavern and Jabu-Jabu no longer have to be finished. *gymnast86* put the saving at about 20 minutes.
- **Video:** [Forest Temple SoT skip by *ontrigger*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wEKZvrajzQE). Tutorial: [*ThreeLetterName*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4V1lRs6J-Sk).

- **Also known as:** irons skip, bow room void, "bomb respawn".
- **What it does:** goes from the east courtyard, where the Song of Time skip leaves Link, straight into the Stalfos room that holds the bow. The flooded well and the Iron Boots it needs are never used. Playing the ocarina in a ceiling corner of the east courtyard sets the respawn point there. A bomb at the centre of that spot makes the game treat it as occupied (it does not have to touch Link), and voiding out (which does not despawn bombs, unlike a reload) moves Link to the next valid place, inside the Stalfos room. Once Link has the bow he can warp out at once: the eye switch does not have to be shot, because the flag that blocks warping is set by walking through the door (*Modern* and *Zwaf*, 16 Nov).

The respawn point stores Link's facing, and facings in the 150s fail (*rqzz*, *Chevy*). [Final setup video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PuthKyzdZU4).

### Collapse Longshot skip

- **Also known as:** Longshot skip, LS skip. The bombchu version is called the Rizz skip, after the cutscene it skips.
- **What it does:** the tower escape has a gap that Link and Zelda cross with the Longshot. Dying at one checkpoint and walking back despawns a flame wall, which opens a way onto the broken arch. From there the plain Hookshot reaches the target.

### Chest minigame storage

- **Also known as:** CMS, chest storage, chest minigame escape, chest minigame warp.
- **What it does:** leaving the Treasure Chest Game in the Market without ending it keeps the game running through loading zones. The next death anywhere returns Link to the chest game, as a child, whatever his age. A death in that state can store other minigames or events in turn, which is how [BOoB](#bottle-out-of-bounds-boob), [fishing storage](#fishing-storage) and [HoFF item smuggling](#hoff-item-smuggling) work.
- **How:** start the chest game at night (before Zelda's Lullaby there is no time of day, so it cannot be done earlier) and get out of the shop with the game still running, by being pushed back through the door as Link targets and talks to the shopkeeper.
- **The standard setup** needs no sword or shield ([*Snugggles*, 25 Nov](https://youtu.be/gxL3-tUaepI), refined by *IsLana* and pinned in the Chest Storage Setup thread): open the door, hold ZL, take out the ocarina and turn the camera until the left edge of the doorframe sits on a dark smudge on the wall panel, put the ocarina away and jump straight out of it, then still holding ZL jump forward and backflip, and retarget and talk as fast as the two inputs of an ocarina actor jump, before the backflip's landing ends (*Smant*). Only that last input is timed. *IsLana* had 20 in a row on video and one fail in more than 60; several runners report it every time on console. It "100% does not work on emulator" (*IsLana*); *dannyb* suspects the frame rate. The neutral jump sets the stored jump height, which the Hyrule Field jump covers again. Other setups: [*Zelduh*'s lineup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Shp9s9tmbDI), menu-buffered turns ([*Zas*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EyTNeYR-S_A), [*TOG2018*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RpaMNJAuRdI), [*jakezsr*](https://youtu.be/d_A3lMS0lKM)), [*Smant*'s slash count](https://www.twitch.tv/smallant/clip/OddAbnegateDragonflySuperVinlin-Nee7IRGLrOheIWSY), [*Smallant*'s setup with a shield](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDqpalb4O1s).
- **Why setups fail** (*mrasmus*, 25 to 26 Nov, scripted emulator runs, preliminary): with position and facing matched to a thousandth, the push still sometimes failed. The one predictor found is a set of tiny leftover rotation values in the physics state, roughly Link's angular velocity: exactly zero failed, about 1e-10 succeeded. He calls it an indicator, not the cause. Releasing and re-pressing target before talking took his shield setup from 85% to 76 in a row. *Volvagia* mapped the talk radius, the targeting break line and the door's push arc with sloperina 0.0.14.
- **Side effects:** Link cannot push or pull blocks until another minigame is started and ended: quitting the slingshot gallery or Dampé's tour works (*Xin*). A separate "game safety" state blocks saving and grabbing in some cases; starting and ending any minigame clears it (*Smant*, *SleepiPanda*), and reloading a save or choosing Try Again after a death sets it again. The state survives the title screen (*Smant*) but not a new game (*jakezsr*).
- **Game-over priority** (*SleepiPanda* and *ontrigger*, from the code, 20 Nov): Dampé, cucco, bombchu bowling, chest game, diving, fishing, then the others. Only the chest game and fishing persist; the rest reset when their hidden actor unloads.
- **Found by:** *Brando♣* and *Leem*, 19 Nov, as the HoFF smuggling setup.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high, as the setup for BOoB. It costs about 1:45 including the trip (*Snugggles*).
- **Thread:** [Chest Minigame Escape](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1572786593020903464).

### Bottle Out of Bounds (BOoB)

- **Also known as:** BOoB storage, bottle OoB, potion seller storage (*Edd*'s sheet), bottle clip, "Matt Mercer summon".
- **What it does:** a clip through almost any wall, once per file, at either age. The Market potion-shop man gives Link a bottle and asks him to catch a bug. If Link escapes the shop with that quest step active, the next bug he catches in a bottle summons the shopkeeper next to him, and the cutscene places Link a fixed distance from him, through the wall. *SleepiPanda*: it is the only event that warps Link relative to an NPC.
- **How:** with chest minigame storage active, enter the potion shop (night is fine; *PancakeTurtle*), take the bottle without picking up the bug, and die inside the shop: bombs, or a cucco storm started at the door so the cuccos keep attacking inside ([*bradyONE*'s setup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u38ABUCtQIc)). The chest game pulls Link out with the quest still active. Then buy a bug (30 rupees in Goron City or Zora's Domain), release it at the clip spot and catch it. Buying a bug does not trigger it; catching one does, so wild bugs cannot be collected meanwhile.
- **Behaviour:** the quest step is saved with the file and survives the title screen and a relaunch, and nothing resets it, so it can be used once per file, but reloading a save made before the bug was caught makes it usable again (*Ben - BJL*, [stream at 1:10:05](https://www.youtube.com/live/e8O1gemL8OM)). Air-wall volumes such as the Gerudo Valley gate box push Link back out, and very thick walls stop it (*SymbaTron*, *Tustin2121*, *nozzly*). After a load or a death in this state no saves or autosaves happen until an event turns saving back on (Dead Hand, Bongo Bongo, Dampé or the slingshot gallery). Loading in or near the Market can put Link back in the potion shop; *SymbaTron* lost a file that way. The red potion it hands over sets the save/load point like the ocarina (*Pointy☭*).
- **Uses shown:** the Shadow Temple from Dead Hand's room onto Bongo Bongo (*jakezsr*, 22 Nov; [setup video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q9M9cZX25D8)), the [trials skip](#trials-skip), child Forest and Fire temples (the Forest one by the wall next to its entrance, no cancels, [*Epher Man*](https://youtu.be/aspAr3MSpeM)), the Door of Time, the Hyrule Castle window guard, the child Bottom of the Well and the child Lon Lon Ranch grotto (*Xin*, *Smant*, 26 Nov), and the Hyrule Castle Song of Storms grotto ([*Wolfram*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8_wXXy7LnU)). It cannot get through the Ice Cavern entrance, and no way into the child Water Temple has been found (a bounty is up). Dodongo's Cavern's bomb wall failed, but *Wolfram* says he has BOoBed into the Cavern.
- **Found by:** *varnish017*, 22 Nov (*Ninpalk* opened the thread; the first clips were posted by *光LightFire*). *Smant* wrote the pinned explanation.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. It skips most of the child Shadow Temple: a little over 5 minutes net by *CrypticJacknife*'s count, up to 8 by *Edd*'s.
- **Video:** [child Shadow Temple by *CrypticJacknife*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XlwgNrAB73U); [1.1.1 by *Liam Pack*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjNTdU0ohis). Thread: [Bottle Out of Bounds](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1573862855717953596).

### Item-wheel frame advance (pause buffering)

- **What it does:** the game pauses while the item wheel or pause menu is open and runs for a few frames between openings. Inputs can be placed on single frames this way.
- **How:** tap the wheel button repeatedly, or pause with Plus, hold the wheel button and unpause, which gives one frame to act before the wheel appears.
- **Found by:** *Caidren*, 5 Nov. The pause-menu version is by *Poodleskirt* (8 Nov). *Yatagarasu* noted that talking to Navi also pauses the game while attack cooldowns keep running.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** medium. Players use it for the frame-tight parts of other glitches, the hookshot storage release for one, and it costs time on every use.

### Hookshot storage

- **Also known as:** HS, hook storage.
- **What it does:** if the hookshot is interrupted on the frame it attaches, the attach point is never cleared. The next hookshot release pulls Link to that stored point, through climbable walls and grates. The stored point survives loading zones, saving and loading, and a change of save file. It reaches about 70 units with either hookshot (*NathanLoto*).
- **How:** place two bombs so the first sets off the second and only the second hits Link. Stand against the wall, hold the hookshot and release on the first frame of the first explosion. *Pizzabite*'s write-up finds that frame by pause buffering. Unequipping the Iron Boots or falling from a slope can also interrupt it. So does taking off the Hover Boots on the frame the hookshot is released, which needs no bombs (*Dusty_Carpet*, 16 Nov; [tutorial by *Zas*](https://youtu.be/F6zoh47n6U4)), and an enemy's attack, such as a Beamos or a Lizalfos (*Dusty_Carpet*, *yume*).

### Actor despawn

- **Also known as:** actor deletion, actor unloading.
- **What it does:** a state reached from the [Geddan boost](#geddan-boost) setup makes the game remove whatever actor Link dies in front of. Doors, NPCs, enemies, Epona and bosses can be deleted.
- **How:** child Link at low health, holding the Giant's Knife or Biggoron Sword, stands against a wall in grass until the grass disappears in two steps; away from grass, wait about 30 seconds. Then, with a fairy in a bottle, die in front of the actor. The state survives saving, loading and a death, and can be carried to another file by loading it. Returning to the title screen or loading an adult save ends it.
- **What it cannot delete:** invisible walls, rocks, bombable walls and push blocks. Deleted enemies do not count as defeated, so a room that waits for them stays locked. Actors come back when the area is reloaded.
- **More** (*Beedo Buddy*, 20 to 23 Nov): fall-damage deaths delete things too (a bomb flower, the Door of Time, Impa, the giant spinning blades), and the ragdoll death state can be moved to another file, so only a fairy is needed there. It can also end bombchu bowling and the cucco game.
- **Minigames:** deleting the door of Talon's cucco game escapes it (30 seconds on the clock, *Xin*); deleting the bombchu bowling door resets the alley but keeps the ten bombchus until a map change (*Toti Sauce*).
- **Found by:** *Beedo Buddy*, 18 Nov.
- **Any% impact:** none. Child Link only gets a two-handed sword after an adult section. *Beedo Buddy* finished the Shadow Temple without Din's Fire by deleting its entrance door.
- **Thread:** [Actor Despawn Glitch](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1572647787282170106).

### Spirit and Shadow Temple skip

- **Also known as:** the HoFF route, Light Arrow smuggle.
- **What it does:** HoFF item smuggling into the Ganon rematch gives the Light Arrows, hammer, Mirror Shield and the other late-game items without beating the Shadow or Spirit temples. *Plyd* had the Light Arrows on a 1.1.1 console on 19 Nov.
- **Why it works** (*SleepiPanda* from the code, *Xin*, *光LightFire*, 19 to 20 Nov): the trials run on a boss counter shared between them. Dying mid-trial in chest storage and coming back skips the current fight, so a trial can be pushed on to fight 9 (Ganondorf) and 10 and 11 (Ganon). Beating Ganon's first phase raises his kill count, which unlocks the master trial. Entering by a main boss statue during a rush keeps the inventory. The Mirror Shield is the only item that merges back into the real inventory.
- **Variants:** a faster first gauntlet that dies to the solo Phantom Ganon (*SleepiPanda*), an ocarina-keeping entry by the Gohma statue (*SleepiPanda*, *Xin*), and [fishing storage](#fishing-storage) to carry the boss counters into the gauntlet, which skips nine fights (*Plyd*).
- **Found by:** *Plyd* (Light Arrows on 19 Nov, thread on 20 Nov), with *SleepiPanda*, *Xin*, *varnish017* and *光LightFire*.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **The child Forest version** (*Plyd*, 25 Nov): BOoB into the Forest Temple and finish it as a child with the [any-age boss key skip](#forest-temple-boss-key-skip-any-age), which drops the Deku Tree, Dodongo's Cavern and the Song of Time and Master Sword cutscenes. It still needs Jabu-Jabu (with the Ruto teleport), one slingshot seed and one bomb; [fishing storage](#fishing-storage) skips eight or nine fights and makes Link adult. *Plyd* called it about 15 minutes faster than the old HoFF route and "at least sub 2" on 1.1.1, unoptimised, and thinks a bombless boss key skip would make it the fastest route on any version.
- **Any% impact:** none yet. By 24 Nov *Edd* put the old version 15 to 20 minutes behind; the child Forest version has not been timed against the main route. It needs the Prelude, plus a wallet and bombs.
- **Video:** [*Plyd*](https://youtu.be/0gAKzhICBQw). Thread: [Spirit and Shadow Temple Skip](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1573206335875584120). Sheets: [*Plyd*'s](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Be5MAzgoIdNUsmZsAiNTUhlHFQ7aYpAR1EvvO2Fu6YQ) and the HoFF tab of *Edd*'s.

### Fishing storage

- **What it does:** with chest storage active, drowning in the fishing pond stores the fishing game too. The first later death goes to the chest game, the next to fishing, which returns Link at his current age, because fishing checks an age flag the Hall does not update (*SleepiPanda*). It locks the item wheel.
- **How** (*光LightFire*): with chest storage active and low health, put a bomb down before talking to the fishing man and walk back into it, or drown stuck under the log. Child Link drowns by crouching with the Hylian Shield (*Xin*). Doing chest storage again gives the item wheel back.
- **Found by:** *varnish017*, 20 Nov; *Xin* first drowned out of fishing on 19 Nov.
- **Any% impact:** none. It is part of the HoFF route.

### Trials skip

- **What it does:** a [BOoB](#bottle-out-of-bounds-boob) clip in Ganon's Castle puts Link out of bounds. Jumpslash cancels take him diagonally over the entrance room, the Beamos room and the archway to the top of the castle, where he sets the respawn (red potion, ocarina or Farore's Wind) and voids or reloads. All six trials are skipped; the last double Iron Knuckle fight is not.
- **Found by:** *Pointy☭* and *ontrigger*, 22 Nov (*ontrigger*'s [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oD-ssyRh3HY)). *Ymuy* did it on console the same day. Save before the clip: the ceiling can catch Link.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** none. BOoB works once per file, and the Shadow Temple use saves more while the Hover Boots make the trials fast. It would skip the bombchus and the Golden Gauntlets, so it matters for low% and HoFF routes.
- **Thread:** [Trials Skip (BoOB)](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1574176975596032000).

### Forest Temple boss key skip (any age)

- **What it does:** reaches Phantom Ganon without the boss key, hookshot storage or the Iron Boots, as child or adult, so it works on 1.1.1. A child who beats him turns adult.
- **How** (*Peterson24246*): enter (a child by BOoB), do the Song of Time skip, straighten the red hallway with the slingshot, invalid-respawn into the bow room, go back to the red staircase room, whose top door is out of bounds because the room was turned early, drop into the well water out of bounds, swim to the arena, then save and reload into it. For a child the [child Song of Time skip](#smaller-finds) replaces the adult one.
- **Found by:** *Peterson24246*, 23 Nov. *Pasty* and *Pointy☭* confirmed it on 1.1.1, *Pointy☭* also as an adult.
- **Any% impact:** none. The route does not finish the Forest Temple, and *nozzly* read in the code that finishing it after the Shadow and Spirit temples blocks the Light Arrow cutscene (untested). It matters for All Dungeons and the HoFF route.
- **Thread:** [Forest Temple Boss Key Skip (Any Age)](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1574290208143450122).

### Ragdoll warping

- **What it does:** after some Hall of Fallen Foes game-overs, Link is pulled in a straight line towards where his ragdoll lay in the arena, on whatever map he respawns on. The state persists through warps, saves and loads; a bomb tumble or the title screen ends it.
- **Why** (*ontrigger*, AI-assisted reading of the code): the health refill ends the ragdoll and restores Link's collision six frames before the respawn. An enemy hitbox that touches him on the second-to-last frame is processed on the last one, so he respawns ragdolled with no damage, and the ragdoll's position is never reset. Which frame the hit lands on depends on where the limbs fell, so it cannot be timed by inputs.
- **Without the Hall** (*ontrigger*, 25 Nov): a death with chest storage active works too, but only as a child and outside the Market, and only from a hit that forces a ragdoll; ages cannot be mixed. Dampé's game works but cannot be escaped, fishing crashed. [Child version in the Shadow Temple by *Dusty_Carpet*](https://youtu.be/h3JAQvGW1QA). It is still not reproducible on demand: the ragdoll's resting place is not deterministic.
- **Found by:** *ontrigger*, 21 Nov, explaining a Morpha clip and an earlier one by *Bluey*. *Dusty_Carpet* got it against Iron Knuckles.
- **Works on:** 1.0. On 1.1.1 the ragdoll's speed is capped, so Link drifts slowly towards the spot instead of warping (*ontrigger*, 24 Nov).
- **Any% impact:** none yet. A trials skip through it is speculative.
- **Video:** [*Dusty_Carpet*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSHqG0mPODg). Thread: [Ragdoll Warping](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1573807036653633820).

### Jabu Ruto teleport

- **What it does:** Ruto respawns only where there is water. Dropping and re-grabbing her as she slides teleports Link, and he clips out of bounds by a door. About 50 jumpslash cancels, or a void, then reach the room before Barinade or the boomerang room. It skips the tentacles and Big Octo. Barinade still needs the boomerang, and he spawns only once his door opens.
- **Found by:** the method follows the cucco teleport (thread by *Piggachew*, 12 Nov). *Dusty_Carpet* made the route version on 20 to 21 Nov ([video](https://youtu.be/UAn2MOkK0hM)); setups by *Wolfram* and *sep*.
- **Works on:** 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** none, because the route skips Jabu-Jabu. About 3.5 minutes saved where Jabu is done (*bradyONE*); Child Dungeons uses it, for about 5 minutes ([*Wolfram*'s 59:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09-n162qmG4), 24 Nov). It can leave Ruto broken and corrupted one file.
- **Thread:** [Jabu Ruto Teleport](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1570427166238318612).

### Fall damage cancel

- **What it does:** fall damage is measured from the stored jump height, the same value that caps the [jumpslash cancel](#jumpslash-cancel). Set that height below the landing spot with a jump or jump slash, then jump slash at any point during a long fall: Link lands with no damage.

- **Any% impact:** none.

### Hitstop launches

- **Also known as:** hitstop actor jump, enemy-hit launches.
- **What it does:** landing a hit freezes Link for a few frames. Doing an actor jump during that freeze, for example hitting a Poe with the hammer while standing on a moving gravestone, gives a much larger launch. *RoboRotembo* posted it in *Heather!*'s thread about earlier unexplained launches off Stalfos and Iron Knuckles.
- **How:** stand on an actor and hit an enemy. During the freeze, swap items through the wheel or the pause menu, hold ZL and jump on the first frame out of the menu. The hammer, a Deku Stick, the bow and the sword all work. The height depends on which frame of the attack connects and how close the enemy is.
- **Found by:** the first clip, by *RoyArashi*, was shared on 5 Nov and *Heather!* opened a thread on 7 Nov. *RoboRotembo* tied it to hitstop on 16 Nov, refining a streamer's Forest Temple elevator clip posted on Twitter.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1, both confirmed on console.
- **Any% impact:** none. It gives height with no limit, but the collision above is closed wherever it was tried, and no route use had been found by 24 Nov.
- **Video:** [Hammer Hitstop Actor Jump by *RoboRotembo*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QbIiTSNh678).

## Glossary

- **Actor:** any object the game simulates separately from the level: chests, boxes, gravestones, enemies.
- **BK:** boss key.
- **BOoB:** Bottle Out of Bounds. **CMS:** chest minigame storage.
- **DoT:** Door of Time. **ToT:** Temple of Time. **SoT:** Song of Time.
- **HoFF:** Hall of Fallen Foes, the boss rematch mode. It opens for child Link at night once he knows the Prelude of Light, which comes after the Forest Temple.
- **HS:** hookshot storage.
- **JSC / IJSC:** jumpslash cancel, and a chain of them.
- **LACS:** the Light Arrow cutscene.
- **NG+:** a run that depends on something set up in an earlier playthrough.
- **OoB:** out of bounds.
- **Void plane:** a place that respawns Link when he enters it. Most are not surfaces. Each map has a grid of cells marked in or out of bounds, and Link voids in a cell marked far enough out, or when the nearest cell is 8 m or more away (*Nova*, *ontrigger*, *Volvagia*, 17 Nov). The "fence" is the safe area around Link that these checks use; it updates as he moves, and sprinting can skip a cell ([*MutantAura*'s video](https://youtu.be/9L-yAPqC0YE)).
