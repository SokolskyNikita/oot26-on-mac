# Ocarina of Time (Switch 2) glitches

The glitches known in the 2026 Ocarina of Time remake as of 19 November 2026.

- **Sources:** the Ocarina of Time 2026 speedrunning Discord (glitch-hunting channel, the routing forum and the per-glitch video threads), the ZSR wiki, StratDB, YouTube videos and run commentary. Links are in [Sources](#sources).
- **Dates** are UTC, usually the day the first clip or thread was posted.
- **Credits** use the names people post under. Where a thread or video names a finder, that is what is listed.
- **"Works on"** lists the versions someone reported it working on, from thread tags and posts. "1.0 only" means it was reported not to work on 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact** is for runs from a fresh file (no NG+), judged against the route in use on 19 November.

## How the route got here

### The normal game

1. **Child:** Deku Tree, meet Zelda, Dodongo's Cavern, Jabu-Jabu. The Goron's Ruby and Zora's Sapphire trigger Zelda's escape, which gives the Ocarina of Time and the Song of Time. The song and all three stones open the Door of Time, and pulling the Master Sword makes Link an adult.
2. **Adult:** Hookshot from Dampé, then the Forest Temple (bow), Fire Temple (Megaton Hammer), Ice Cavern (Iron Boots) and Water Temple (Longshot). Finishing Forest, Fire and Water unlocks the Nocturne of Shadow, the warp to the Shadow Temple. The Spirit Temple needs a trip back to childhood.
3. **End:** the Shadow and Spirit medallions trigger the Light Arrow cutscene and the bridge to Ganondorf's Castle. Then six trials, Ganondorf, the tower collapse (which needs the Longshot) and Ganon.

### The route around 9 Nov (about 3:02)

Still all three child dungeons, the Door of Time and the Master Sword. The savings were all on the adult side:

- **Shadow Temple without the Nocturne.** An actor jump or jumpslash cancel reaches the entrance directly, so the Forest, Fire and Water temples no longer had to be finished.
- **Those temples were still entered** for their items: bow, hammer and Longshot. The Ice Cavern was still needed for the Iron Boots.
- **Spirit Temple from the adult side only**, jumping straight to the Mirror Shield.

### What changed after that

- **Shadow Temple as a child (in runs by 12 Nov, 2:51).** The puberty skip had been known since 6 Nov but needed a way to get child Link through the temple, which jumpslash cancels gave. It replaced the Door of Time, the Master Sword cutscenes and the walk there. All three child dungeons stayed, because the Song of Time was still needed for one block in the Forest Temple.
- **Song of Time skip (12 Nov, 2:29).** With that block passable, the song was not needed at all. Dodongo's Cavern is now entered only for the bomb bag, and Jabu-Jabu is skipped. About 20 minutes.
- **Longshot and Iron Boots skips (14 Nov, 2:16).** The Longshot was needed for the tower collapse, the Fire trial and the Spirit Temple boss door, and all three got skips. The Iron Boots were needed to enter the Water Temple. After the Song of Time skip they were also needed inside the Forest Temple, which does not need them in a normal playthrough. The skip brings Link into the east courtyard first. The switch that drains the well is in the west courtyard, whose doors are barred from the outside, so the well was still flooded and the boots were the only way through it. The Iron Boots skip bypasses the well. Runs also used the boots for the underwater shortcut between Zora's River and the Lost Woods, but only to save travel. The Ice Cavern and Water Temple dropped out. About 20 minutes.
- **Slingshot skip and the Deku Tree cutscene skip (16 to 17 Nov; the record fell to 2:01:34).** The slingshot was used on Gohma, a Bongo Bongo hand and the skull check in the Shadow Temple, and each has a replacement: sticks and nuts, bombs, bombs. *Bananiidani* put the saving at about 50 seconds at the Deku Tree, less by the end of the Shadow Temple. Skipping the Deku Tree cutscene also removes the Gerudo Valley intro, which checks that cutscene's flag: 28 seconds together, timed by *nozzly*.
- **No more title-screen resets for jump height (18 Nov).** Runners place ordinary jumps where the stored height is enough for the next hover: one in Hyrule Field, one on the way to the Sacred Forest Meadow, one after Dodongo's Cavern and one on a Kakariko roof, which covers the whole Shadow Temple. A reset cost about 13 seconds.
- **Fire maze skip (18 Nov, 1.0 only).** A hookshot storage clip in the Fire Temple skips three small keys on the way to the hammer. Runners measured between 1:06 and 1:46 saved.

### The route now (19 Nov)

- **Fastest run found:** [2:01:34 by *Rick Schmidty*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WqqWSPnmpeU), posted 17 November, on version 1.0. His earlier [2:05:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Wxm7craXQA) of 15 November had 24 seconds added by his own choice, because digital 1.0 loads faster than a cartridge; no rule sets that. *gymnast86* measured the difference at 27.75 seconds over one run. A [2:05 by *iwabi*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OzocTJSSjsI) is the second fastest run linked, and was asked about as the best on 1.1.1.
- **Child:** Deku Tree (cutscene skip, B1 skip, no slingshot, Gohma with four sticks and a nut), ocarina from Saria, market bottle, Zelda's Lullaby, Saria's Song, Goron's Bracelet, bomb bag from Dodongo's Cavern, magic and Din's Fire from the Death Mountain Crater fairy, then the Shadow Temple, which ends with Link as an adult. Bongo Bongo is a two-cycle fight for those who route six Deku Sticks to it.
- **Adult:** Hookshot, Goron Tunic, Fire Temple as far as the hammer (with the fire maze skip on 1.0), Bolero of Fire to leave, Gerudo Fortress through the jail, straight to the Mirror Shield, Spirit Temple and Twinrova, Forest Temple for the bow (Song of Time skip, then the Iron Boots skip, then warp out), Light Arrow cutscene, six trials, Ganondorf, the collapse with the Longshot skip, Ganon.
- **Forest or Spirit first:** undecided, and within a few seconds either way. Spirit first gives the Mirror Shield for *sva*'s Iron Boots skip setup, but shieldless setups exist now. Forest first gives the bow for the Gerudo guard and uses the magic refill better, and *Edd* prefers it on 1.1.1.
- **Hover Boots:** optional on 1.0. Getting them (the Dead Hand fight) takes about 1:40 and they save about 1:16 elsewhere, so skipping them is about 20 seconds faster and harder. On 1.1.1 they are still needed in the Fire Temple.
- **Still required:** all six trials, a bottle (only for blue fire in the Water trial) and the Light Arrow cutscene, which also hands over the Master Sword.
- **Version differences:** 1.0 adds the Spirit boss key skip and the fire maze skip, both built on hookshot storage. *nozzly* put a 1.0 cartridge at about a minute faster than 1.1.1 before the fire maze skip was found.
- **Being researched:** the [Bolero skip](#bolero-skip-and-goron-tunic-skip) with a Goron Tunic skip, a Hookshot skip on 1.1.1, and the [Flare Dancer jump interrupt](#boss-quick-kills).
- **Route sheets:** [*Edd*'s](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1auZa0EjOcMyq6_auf2xWGbtDjQ7K2WaOL1UvZ0Kl7ts) and [*yume*'s route calculator](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1lYlgyrrJ5qzdUDk2soTDEwY7WCFnEYTSuZM6xhnUUaY).
- **Leaderboard rules:** not settled. Whether 1.0 and the current version share a board, and whether amiibo are allowed, were still open on 19 November.

## High impact

### Jumpslash cancel

- **Also known as:** JSC, IJSC (infinite jumpslash cancel), IJS, infinite jump, infinite height glitch, super jump, sword jump, Deku Stick jump, hammer hover, hovering.
- **What it does:** a jump slash started in mid-air gives Link the take-off speed of a fresh 0.7 m jump, as long as he is still rising. A slash cannot restart itself, but changing the item on the weapon button ends it at once and Link drops into a fall, and from a fall the next press starts a new jump slash. So the chain climbs for as long as it is kept up.
- **How:** target and jump slash with a Deku Stick or the Megaton Hammer, open the item wheel, pick another item, let the game run for at least one frame (the slash ends on the first running frame after the wheel closes), pick the weapon again, close the wheel and press the weapon button, then reopen the wheel before Link stops rising. Repeat. The weapon only has to be back on its button, not drawn. The sword does not work: a sword slash cannot be ended in the air.
- **Height cap:** a normal jump stores the height it will peak at (take-off height + 0.7 m), and mid-air jump slashes lift Link up to that height and no further. In the game's code a sidehop sets the same cap; backflips and jump slashes from the ground do not. Returning to the title screen clears it, and so does any change of age (*Poodleskirt*, 17 Nov), which includes the blue warps of the puberty skip. The cap is an absolute height that carries between maps, and maps sit at different heights: a jump in Hyrule Field sets a cap high enough to hover to the sky in Gerudo Fortress. Route sheets mark where to jump before each hover so the stored height is enough for it, and fights on the way are done without jumping to keep it. The same stored height is what the [fall damage cancel](#fall-damage-cancel) uses.
- **Gain per cancel:** in the game's code it is set by how many frames the game runs between two slashes: 0.43 m with 4, 0.59 m with 7, up to 0.7 m with 12, and nothing once Link has stopped rising.
- **Menu order:** sorting the item wheel by most recent makes the menu inputs the same on every cancel (*Edd* and *nozzly*, 16 Nov).
- **Variants:**
  - *Backwards jumpslash* (*bloonstd6master*, 10 Nov): with no sword equipped, target, hold back, press A and tap ZL twice. The ZSR wiki starts it from a backhop and releases the target button once the chain is going. Link hovers while moving backwards, which gains a lot of height in a small space, and holding target again turns it back into a forward hover three cancels later. The first Forest Escape setup used it.
  - *Faster start* (*Kenny-Dumminik*, 11 Nov): pressing the weapon button and A together while holding forward starts the hover without a first swing. *Kenny-Dumminik* wrote that the timing might be frame perfect.
  - *Pause method* (*flutterdashie64*, 8 Nov): the same chain driven by the pause button. In the game's code the pause menu changes the item through the same routine as the wheel.
  - *Higher hover* (*Tholive_* and *Ataappi*): a jump slash lets a Hover Boots hover start above the platform.
- **Found by:** *Ataappi*, 7 Nov. The mechanics were written up by *nathanisbored*.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. Hovering to the Shadow Temple entrance removed the need for the Nocturne of Shadow, and so for finishing the Forest, Fire and Water temples. It is also how child Link gets through that temple, and the Song of Time skip, Iron Boots skip, Longshot skip and Forest Escape all start with a hover.
- **Video:** [Jumpslash Cancel Glitch Tutorial by *gymnast86*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4HSZ6GMQCZk). Inputs with a hand camera: [*secondbaker*'s link](https://youtu.be/aaEfLhigIws).
- **As a script:** [research/infinite-jumpslash](research/infinite-jumpslash/README.md): the chain at 50 frames a loop (0.69 m up each), played inside the game by sloperinaGZ, with each timing value's window and the height formula.

### Actor jump

- **Also known as:** actor bouncing, ocarina jump, bean jump, bean flower jump, chest jump, child super jump, hookshot jump.
- **What it does:** jumping on the frame an aim or ocarina animation ends, while standing on a movable or interactive object, launches Link far higher than a normal jump. A moving object adds its speed.
- **How:** stand on an actor (chest, gravestone, box, block, bean plant), hold target, take out the ocarina, turn the camera all the way down, put the ocarina away and press jump a moment later. The hookshot, bow, slingshot and boomerang work in place of the ocarina. Child Link can do it with only the Hylian Shield, and *RoboRotembo* showed on 9 Nov that opening the item wheel works with no item at all. *Edd*'s summary (18 Nov) is that anything that forces the camera to snap works: the pause menu, a bottle, putting away an aimed hookshot. Hookshot jumps are shorter, and child Link gets less height than adult Link.
- **Variants:**
  - *Bean jump* (*Vidya James*, 6 Nov): the first form found, off a moving bean plant.
  - *Directional actor jump* (*Hellios*, 10 Nov): releasing the ocarina quickly steers the launch.
  - *Hitstop actor jump* (*RoboRotembo*, 16 Nov): see [Hitstop launches](#hitstop-launches).
  - *Big chests:* an open big chest touches Link on three sides and gives a larger launch, which depends on lineup and direction (*RoboRotembo*, *Vespher*).
- **Found by:** *Vidya James* (bean jump), then *Ataappi* showed it works on any actor and with the shield alone.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. It was the first way into the Shadow Temple early and to the Mirror Shield and Silver Gauntlets. Much of that is now done with jumpslash cancels. Runs still use actor jumps in the Fire Temple and, off a chest in the Shadow trial, to skip lifting the Golden Gauntlets block ([tutorial by *parallelograms*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yjS5MQpx4Tw)).
- **Video:** [Actor Jumping by *Kethaldo*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rop0oeZVk24). A fixed setup: [Bean Jump for Shadow Temple Early by *RoboRotembo*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1EIvnoPTQ48).

### Puberty skip

- **Also known as:** child skip, early adult, "the Door of Time skip".
- **What it does:** finishing an adult dungeon as child Link plays the sage cutscene and returns Link to the overworld as an adult, with the Kokiri Sword still equipped. The Door of Time never has to open. The Master Sword then arrives through the Light Arrow cutscene, which checks only for the Shadow and Spirit medallions.
- **Why it works:** the Shadow Temple's blue warp always loads the adult Graveyard map, and a map forces Link's age to match it. Current age and "has done the Master Sword sequence" are tracked separately, so Link is adult without that sequence ever running. *leo* found the setting in the map data (17 Nov): overworld maps are marked to force the age, dungeons and the Hall of Fallen Foes are not. *MaddoScientisto* and *Nova* read the blue warp scripts: every adult dungeon's warp makes a child adult, and only the Dodongo's Cavern warp checks age. Leaving the Gerudo Training Ground as a child also turns Link adult, because Gerudo Fortress has no child map.
- **Found by:** *oussama* and *tvbrayden*, per the ZSR wiki. *kittenchilly* mentioned it on 6 Nov and *tvbrayden* wrote it up on 7 Nov.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. On its own it replaced the Door of Time and Master Sword sequence. With it in the route the Spiritual Stones had one use left, the Song of Time, which is why the Song of Time skip could remove two child dungeons.
- **Video:** the [2:01:34 run](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WqqWSPnmpeU).

### Save/load respawn manipulation

- **Also known as:** save warp, save/load clip, out of bounds to in bounds, loading a file in mid-air.
- **What it does:** a save stores Link's last safe position, and a load runs a second, stricter check. If Link saves somewhere the game does not accept, the load places him at a nearby valid spot, which can be on the other side of a wall. Playing the ocarina out of bounds pins the save position there.
- **How the game picks the spot** (*RoboRotembo* and *ontrigger*, 17 Nov): the respawn point follows Link only on stable ground in bounds, and the ocarina or a cutscene item forces it to update anywhere. The game then looks for the nearest valid spot to that point, preferring in bounds over out of bounds. So the trick can bring Link from out of bounds to in bounds through a wall, and cannot send him out. Farore's Wind set out of bounds behaves like the ocarina.
- **Found by:** *wxyz* (9 Nov, out of bounds back in bounds), *GlitchesAndStuff* (9 Nov, loading in mid-air), *Poodleskirt* (10 Nov, loading into a place never visited), *Dusty_Carpet* (13 Nov, ocarina pins the position).
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. The Song of Time skip and the Iron Boots skip are both built on it.
- **Video:** [Ocarina saving our position from out of bounds by *Dusty_Carpet*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRGNLxWWfDc).

### Bomb T-pose

- **Also known as:** bomb pose (the ZSR wiki's name), bomb/chu T-pose, bomb clip.
- **What it does:** putting the ocarina away while Link is breathing in leaves his upper-body animation in a wider state. Pulling a bomb or bombchu while moving then swings his arms out and pushes him through nearby collision.
- **How:** hold any note until it wobbles (3 seconds or more), release it, wait half a second, put the ocarina away, then pull a bomb while moving. *Nova* found that any of Link's inhales works if the put-away is timed to it. Jumping, climbing, rolling, sliding, throwing or picking up a bomb, pulling one while standing still or taking damage ends the state.
- **Found by:** *Pasty* (*PastyPixel*), 12 Nov.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high, as the clip used in the Song of Time skip.
- **Video:** [Any% Is Now 20 Minutes Faster by *ThreeLetterName*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4V1lRs6J-Sk), which shows the T-pose and the skip.

### Song of Time skip

- **Also known as:** SoT skip, SoT block skip.
- **What it does:** the bow is in the Forest Temple, behind a block that only the Song of Time removes. This skip reaches the east courtyard without the song. Hover to the top of a column in the main room, save, do the bomb T-pose to wedge Link into the corner, save again and reload: Link loads into the courtyard on the other side of the wall. The bow is reached from the west courtyard, across a flooded well.
- **Found by:** *Ultraspeed7* found the save/load spot (11 Nov), *Pasty* the T-pose, and *ontrigger* posted the first full clip on 12 Nov. Later setups by *Lid*, *Layz*, *jakezsr* and *CrypticJacknife* cut the hover from about 60 cancels to under 20, and a "zero-lineup" setup needs about 6 early-timed cancels.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. Learning the song takes the Goron's Ruby and Zora's Sapphire, and this block was its only use, so Dodongo's Cavern and Jabu-Jabu no longer have to be finished. *gymnast86* put the saving at about 20 minutes.
- **Video:** [Forest Temple SoT skip by *ontrigger*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wEKZvrajzQE). Tutorial: [*ThreeLetterName*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4V1lRs6J-Sk).

### Iron Boots skip (Invalid Respawn)

- **Also known as:** irons skip, bow room void, "bomb respawn".
- **What it does:** goes from the east courtyard, where the Song of Time skip leaves Link, straight into the Stalfos room that holds the bow. The flooded well and the Iron Boots it needs are never used. Playing the ocarina in a ceiling corner of the east courtyard sets the respawn point there. A bomb at the centre of that spot makes the game treat it as occupied (it does not have to touch Link), and voiding out (which does not despawn bombs, unlike a reload) moves Link to the next valid place, inside the Stalfos room. Once Link has the bow he can warp out at once: the eye switch does not have to be shot, because the flag that blocks warping is set by walking through the door (*Modern* and *Zwaf*, 16 Nov).
- **Found by:** *Ultraspeed7*, 14 Nov. Bomb-throw lineup by *DrDiabeteus*. The setup with shield turns and a single bomb throw is by *sva* (15 Nov). *Fat Albert* called it the most consistent of the setups tried, and *CrypticJacknife* and *squimmie* endorsed it. Setups without a shield followed on 16 Nov: a bomb throw by *minimini352* and a Kokiri Sword lineup by *karterfreak*.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. The Iron Boots come from the Ice Cavern and were also needed to enter the Water Temple for the Longshot. With the Longshot skip, both places drop out of the route. *gymnast86* put the saving at another 20 minutes.
- **Video:** [Iron Boots Skip from Actor Jump](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pyZic3Uyp08). Without Hookshot: [Song of Time Skip (No Iron Boots / Hookshot) by *karterfreak*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RyWAgiSeS9M). Shieldless setup: [Bomb throw setup by *minimini352*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xscphRUG_A).

### Collapse Longshot skip

- **Also known as:** Longshot skip, LS skip. The bombchu version is called the Rizz skip, after the cutscene it skips.
- **What it does:** the tower escape has a gap that Link and Zelda cross with the Longshot. Dying at one checkpoint and walking back despawns a flame wall, which opens a way onto the broken arch. From there the plain Hookshot reaches the target.
- **Two methods:**

## Medium impact

### Item-wheel frame advance (pause buffering)

- **What it does:** the game pauses while the item wheel or pause menu is open and runs for a few frames between openings. Inputs can be placed on single frames this way.
- **How:** tap the wheel button repeatedly, or pause with Plus, hold the wheel button and unpause, which gives one frame to act before the wheel appears.
- **Found by:** *Caidren*, 5 Nov. The pause-menu version is by *Poodleskirt* (8 Nov). *Yatagarasu* noted that talking to Navi also pauses the game while attack cooldowns keep running.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** medium. Players use it for the frame-tight parts of other glitches, the hookshot storage release for one, and it costs time on every use.

### Hookshot storage

- **Also known as:** HS, hook storage.
- **What it does:** if the hookshot is interrupted on the frame it attaches, the attach point is never cleared. The next hookshot release pulls Link to that stored point, through climbable walls and grates. The stored point survives loading zones, saving and loading, and a change of save file. It reaches about 70 units with either hookshot (*NathanLoto*).
- **How:** place two bombs so the first sets off the second and only the second hits Link. Stand against the wall, hold the hookshot and release on the first frame of the first explosion. *Pizzabite*'s write-up finds that frame by pause buffering. Unequipping the Iron Boots or falling from a slope can also interrupt it. So does taking off the Hover Boots on the frame the hookshot is released, which needs no bombs (*Dusty_Carpet*, 16 Nov; [tutorial by *Zas*](https://youtu.be/F6zoh47n6U4)), and an enemy's attack, such as a Beamos or a Lizalfos (*Dusty_Carpet*, *yume*).
- **Uses:** clips behind ladders and grates, the [boss key skips](#boss-key-skips) and fast travel across a map (*fast hookshot storage movement*, *Dusty_Carpet*). Cancelling a storage pull keeps its speed as a launch: *NathanLoto* measured about 29 during the pull and about 66 after the cancel. It works on any actor, including push blocks, gibdos and redeads, which the hookshot cannot normally target.
- **Found by:** *NathanLoto*, 8 Nov. The two-bomb setup is by *GlitchesAndStuff*.
- **Works on:** 1.0 only. Releasing with the target unloaded, or on another map, crashes the game. *FantaTanked* and *ontrigger* compared the code of the two versions (17 Nov, with AI help, not checked in game): in 1.0 the hook's attached state keeps following its anchor after the hook is gone, and 1.1.1 ends the state when the hooked object no longer exists.
- **Any% impact:** medium, and only on 1.0: the Spirit boss key skip and the fire maze skip.
- **Video:** [Hookshot Storage Setup by *Pizzabite*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sgmJNYPChXc).

None of these is in the fresh-file Any% route. They need NG+, are slower than the alternative or have no use yet.

### Yiga Warp

- **Also known as:** save location transfer, redead warp.
- **What it does:** returning to the title screen from the redead bridge in the tower collapse leaves the escape checkpoint position marked valid. After that, a manual save and reload in any area places Link at those coordinates on the current map. *Plyd* mapped where it lands: above the Fire Temple boss room and above the Water Temple switch room. The positions are fixed per map and listed in [*Brando♣*'s sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1rRUlhlRFeat1hX2U-A2uBH-KP9cXym2mwsXtoD4dF8Q); most are void loops. Autosaves stay off while the state is active.
- **Found by:** *Ymuy*, 14 Nov. Mechanism by *ontrigger*.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** none for fresh-file runs, because the escape has to be reached first. Asked whether that makes it an NG+ trick, *Plyd* said yes.
- **Video:** [YigaBomb/Load by *Brando*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ZN8M4rvC2Y). [Yiga Warp and Dog Delay overview by *Kyle_Speedruns*](https://youtu.be/naBQRvwIa68).

### Bolero skip and Goron Tunic skip

The Bolero of Fire cutscene never plays, and entering the temple removes its trigger. The same entry was shown without the Goron Tunic, using the [heat invulnerability](#smaller-finds) from a crater grotto and a red potion.

### Actor despawn

- **Also known as:** actor deletion, actor unloading.
- **What it does:** a state reached from the [Geddan boost](#geddan-boost) setup makes the game remove whatever actor Link dies in front of. Doors, NPCs, enemies, Epona and bosses can be deleted.
- **How:** child Link at low health, holding the Giant's Knife or Biggoron Sword, stands against a wall in grass until the grass disappears in two steps; away from grass, wait about 30 seconds. Then, with a fairy in a bottle, die in front of the actor. The state survives saving, loading and a death, and can be carried to another file by loading it. Returning to the title screen or loading an adult save ends it.
- **What it cannot delete:** invisible walls, rocks, bombable walls and push blocks. Deleted enemies do not count as defeated, so a room that waits for them stays locked. Actors come back when the area is reloaded.
- **Found by:** *Beedo Buddy*, 18 Nov.
- **Any% impact:** none. Child Link only gets a two-handed sword after an adult section. *Beedo Buddy* finished the Shadow Temple without Din's Fire by deleting its entrance door.
- **Thread:** [Actor Despawn Glitch](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1572647787282170106).

### HoFF item smuggling

- **Also known as:** chest minigame escape.

Dying in a Hall of Fallen Foes fight in that state brings the fight's fixed loadout back with him. It replaces Link's items apart from key items, and the ocarina is lost.
- **How:** start the chest game, get out of the shop with the game still running, enter a fight in the Hall of Fallen Foes and die. The first way out was to delete the shop door with the [actor despawn](#actor-despawn). - **What the fights give:** for example bow, Longshot and Iron Boots from Bongo Bongo, and Golden Gauntlets from the two Iron Knuckles. [*Snugggles*'s sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/19nov_gsPDvb2xETzyA0YfNW67a6xz7hPoG0dDFRWPQg) lists them. Saves made in this state can be corrupted.
- **Found by:** *Brando♣* and *Leem*, 19 Nov.
- **Any% impact:** none. The Hall opens only after the Forest Temple is finished and Link has returned to childhood, and only at night (*Pointy☭*), which a file that skipped the Hyrule Field owl never reaches. Nobody had brought out the Light Arrows by 19 Nov.
- **Video:** [Deku Stick and slingshot method by *Snugggles*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I98muayNnq0). Thread: [Chest Minigame Escape](https://discord.com/channels/1513960405364375593/1572786593020903464).

### Platform momentum storage

- **What it does:** the speed of a moving platform is kept after leaving it, as long as Link lands only on certain ground types. Jumpslash cancels and Hover Boots inherit it. A comparison of the two versions' code by *oussama* (AI-assisted, not tested in game) says 1.1 caps the speed Link takes from a moving surface and 1.0 does not.
- **Found by:** *ontrigger*, 11 Nov.
- **Video:** [platform momentum storage by *ontrigger*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnZz7z6EUVE).

- **Fishing rod steal** (*DobleC*, 11 Nov): unequip the Iron Boots as the fish leaves the water and the rod stays on the item button. Tested on 1.1.1. It carries from adult to child, not from child to adult (*varnish017*).

## Glossary

- **Actor:** any object the game simulates separately from the level: chests, boxes, gravestones, enemies.
- **BK:** boss key.
- **DoT:** Door of Time. **ToT:** Temple of Time. **SoT:** Song of Time.
- **HoFF:** Hall of Fallen Foes, the boss rematch mode. It opens after the Forest Temple, for child Link.
- **HS:** hookshot storage.
- **JSC / IJSC:** jumpslash cancel, and a chain of them.
- **LACS:** the Light Arrow cutscene.
- **NG+:** a run that depends on something set up in an earlier playthrough.
- **OoB:** out of bounds.
- **Void plane:** a place that respawns Link when he enters it. Most are not surfaces. Each map has a grid of cells marked in or out of bounds, and Link voids in a cell marked far enough out, or when the nearest cell is 8 m or more away (*Nova*, *ontrigger*, *Volvagia*, 17 Nov).
